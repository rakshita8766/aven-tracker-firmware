1. Check SIM Status
If the SIM is locked or not detected, it will search forever.
Command: AT+CPIN?
Goal: You want to see +CPIN: READY.
2. Set the APN
LTE and NB-IoT require an APN to complete the registration process. Without it, the network often denies the "Attach" request.
Command: AT+CGDCONT=1,"IP","your_apn"
(Replace your_apn with your carrier's APN, e.g., "internet", "hologram", or "mnet.iot")
3. Scan for Available Operators
If the above doesn't work, see what networks the modem can actually "see" nearby. This confirms if your SIM is compatible with the available towers.
Command: AT+COPS=?
Note: This can take 30–120 seconds. Wait for the response. It will return a list of operators like (1,"Airtel","","40445",7).
